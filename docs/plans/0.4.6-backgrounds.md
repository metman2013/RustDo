# Plan: 0.4.6, background images

Requested by a user; agreed with the maintainer on 2026-11-26. To build next.

## What
- **App-wide background** (Settings › Appearance › Background), default: none (today's look).
- **Per-list override** from a list's right-click / long-press menu: Background… with the
  same choices, plus "Use app background".
- **Built-in backgrounds** (about 8), drawn by RustDo (gradients and gentle patterns, light
  and dark variants: dusk, ocean, forest, sand, aurora…). No photo licensing, tiny size.
- **Your own image** (JPG/PNG) on Windows, Linux and the web: RustDo keeps a resized copy
  (JPEG about 1920 px wide, a few hundred KB), so moving the original doesn't break it.
- **Dim slider** for readability; task rows and side panels become slightly see-through
  cards so text stays readable in light and dark themes. One dim setting for all.

## Sync (Sync my devices)
- Built-in choices, app-wide and per list, sync (just a name).
- Own images sync too, through the Hub, as their own account items (resized copy), so a
  picture chosen on the PC shows on the phone. Hub request limit is 16 MB: fine.
- Android shows synced custom images; picking one on Android needs the photo picker (later).
- Shared family lists: a list's background is personal (like My Day), never sent through
  the share code.
