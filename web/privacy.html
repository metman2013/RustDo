RustDo privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

RustDo is a to-do and sticky notes app that keeps your data on your own devices. In short: RustDo has no accounts, collects no personal data, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. This applies to every version: Windows (including the Microsoft Store version), Linux, Android and the web app at rustdo.app.

What RustDo stores, and where

  • Your tasks, lists, settings and sticky notes are saved on your device: in your Documents folder (Documents\RustDo) on Windows and Linux, in the app's private storage on Android, and in your browser's storage for the web app.
  • Backups are saved on your device, plus any folder you choose yourself.
  • Log files (Windows and Linux) record when RustDo starts and any errors. They stay on your device and are only shared if you copy them and send them yourself (Settings › Help › Copy diagnostic info).

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

When RustDo connects to the internet

  • Update check (Windows installer and portable versions, Linux, Android): RustDo asks GitHub for the latest RustDo version, at most once a day or when you open it, and you can turn this off in Settings. No task data is sent; GitHub sees an ordinary web request from your device. The Microsoft Store version doesn't do this: the Store updates it.
  • Family sharing (optional): if you share a list, RustDo sends that list's tasks to the RustDo Hub address you enter, which is a server you or your family run on your own network. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and lists you don't share stay on your device.
  • Importing from Microsoft To Do (optional): if you choose Sign in with Microsoft, you sign in on Microsoft's own page and RustDo reads your Microsoft To Do lists and tasks once, straight from Microsoft to your device, with read-only access. RustDo doesn't keep the sign-in and doesn't send your tasks anywhere else. Microsoft's privacy statement covers the sign-in.
  • Your own RustDo Hub (optional): with Sync my devices turned on, your lists, tasks, sticky notes and a few settings are sent to the hub address you enter, a server you run yourself, so your devices stay the same.
  • Links you click (release notes, the RustDo Discord, help pages) open in your web browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

Children

RustDo doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because your data is on your device, you control it: you can export it, restore it, or delete it by deleting the RustDo folder (or the app's data on Android, or the site's data in your browser).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/metman2013/RustDo/issues.
